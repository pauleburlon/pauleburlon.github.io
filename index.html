I work on quantitative inequality research — wealth, migration and integration, and political and media discourse. My methodological interest is in measuring what has been hard to measure: building new data sources and computational instruments for substantive questions.

Research

  • 2025–

    Tagesschau corpus & migration salience. Full-text corpus of the spoken Tagesschau um 20 Uhr, 1991–2026, transcribed locally with Whisper. A monthly migration-salience measure, modelled jointly with asylum statistics and public problem awareness.

    Ongoing · 12,522 broadcast days · 01/1991 – 07/2026 · VAR / Granger

  • 2024

    Status maximization via marriage. Which factors shape whether recent male refugees in Germany marry within the host country — and whether they marry partners with higher educational attainment.

    B.A. thesis · Anonymised PARFORM wave 1
