“Status Maximization via Marriage: Refugees’ Partnership Preferences”

B.A. thesis · University of Mannheim · 2024 · Anonymised PARFORM wave 1 · Full sample N = 3,412

Abstract

Examines which factors shape whether recent male refugees in Germany marry within the host country and whether they marry partners with higher educational attainment. The analysis finds that social capital (friendships, time in country) increases marriage probability, while structural-integration markers such as German-language proficiency show null or even negative effects. Employment and education engagement predict upward marital mobility; discrimination remains a barrier.

Findings
